## Lessons Learned

1. **Start simple** - Basic search works best
2. **Shard by 71** - Monster primes are the key
3. **ZK everything** - Proofs enable trust
4. **GPU when needed** - Not always necessary
5. **Parquet format** - Best for columnar data
6. **Unified index** - Single source of truth
7. **Multi-layer** - Redundancy is good
